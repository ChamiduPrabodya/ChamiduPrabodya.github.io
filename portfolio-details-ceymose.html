Custom Storefront for a Small Business

Ceymos Lanka is a lightweight frontend website created to present products for a small shop in a simple and friendly way.

The interface focuses on making products easy to browse by category, helping visitors move through items such as rice, spices, coconut products, and other store goods.

This project was built as a custom solution based on the client's requested functions and provided images, with an emphasis on clean presentation and easy navigation.

Highlights

  • Simple storefront homepage
  • Product category browsing
  • Clean image-based product presentation
  • Lightweight custom frontend build

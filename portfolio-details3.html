Automated Healthcare Trolley System

Auto Rover is an automated trolley concept designed to improve medication delivery inside healthcare environments through guided movement and timed dispensing.

The project combines an Arduino controller, line-following behavior, ultrasonic obstacle sensing, and Bluetooth connectivity to move along predefined paths while responding to its surroundings.

A connected mobile experience supports scheduling and monitoring, helping reduce manual effort and improve reliability in patient-focused delivery tasks.

Highlights

  • Autonomous path-following trolley
  • Obstacle detection with sensors
  • Bluetooth-assisted monitoring

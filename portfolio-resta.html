RESTA-FOOD Restaurant Management System

RESTA-FOOD is a full-stack web application built to streamline restaurant and hotel operations while giving customers a smooth digital ordering experience.

Customers can browse menu items, place dine-in or delivery orders, reserve VIP rooms, manage accounts, and submit feedback. The platform also supports Google sign-in and online payments.

On the management side, the admin dashboard centralizes menu control, bookings, customer records, promotions, and order handling to support day-to-day restaurant operations efficiently.

Key Features

  • Customer registration and login
  • Google sign-in support
  • Online ordering for delivery and dine-in
  • QR-based table ordering
  • VIP room reservations
  • Payment integration and loyalty rewards
  • Admin dashboard for menu and order management

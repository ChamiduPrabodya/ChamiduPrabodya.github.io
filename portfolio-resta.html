RESTA-FOOD Restaurant Management System

RESTA-FOOD is a full-stack restaurant and hotel management web application designed to simplify restaurant operations while providing customers with an easy and modern online ordering experience.

Customers can browse food items, place orders, choose dine-in or delivery options, reserve VIP rooms, provide feedback and manage their accounts. The system also supports Google authentication and online payment functionality.

An administrative dashboard allows restaurant staff to manage menu items, customer orders, bookings, promotions, dining tables, customer information and other restaurant operations through a centralized system.

Key Features

  • Customer registration and login
  • Google Sign-In
  • Online food ordering
  • Delivery and dine-in ordering
  • QR-based table ordering
  • VIP room reservation
  • PayHere payment integration
  • Loyalty points and rewards
  • Customer feedback and reviews
  • Promotion and discount management
  • Admin dashboard
  • Menu and order management
